Privacy

What I collect

If you sign up: your email address, and your phone number only if you choose to give it. I use them to send you the sign-up offer and news about new cases. Texts are sent only if you check the text box. Msg & data rates may apply.

Visit stats: if turned on, I may use simple visit counting to see which pages get visited and which buttons get clicked, such as how many people look at a case. This does not include your name, email or phone number.

Ad pixels: if turned on, I may use ad tools such as TikTok or Meta pixels to see whether visits come from my videos or ads. These tools can set cookies and may share visit data with TikTok or Meta.

This site has no checkout yet, so no payment details are collected here.

Opting out

If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, this site does not load visit stats or ad pixels for you.

To stop emails, reply asking me to unsubscribe, or write to [contact email]. To stop texts, reply STOP to any text. To have your email or number deleted, contact me and I will remove it.
