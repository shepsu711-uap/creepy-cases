Shipping & Policies

Handmade, so please allow time

Every case is sculpted, baked and finished by hand, one at a time. Please allow up to 1 to 2 weeks for your order to be made and shipped. It will get to you. Good weird things take a little while.

Shipping

US only for now. Flat $5 shipping and handling per order. Orders go out by USPS with tracking, and you will get the tracking number as soon as it ships. Once it ships, delivery is usually 2 to 5 business days.

Cases only

These are clay cases only. A lighter is not included, and none are shipped with one. All cases are made for BIC lighters unless a custom order is commissioned for a different lighter.

Returns

Because each case is handmade, all sales are final. If your case arrives broken or damaged, message me within 7 days of delivery with a photo and I will replace it.

Every case is a little different

Handmade clay means small variations in color and detail. The photos show the exact piece you are buying. Colors can look a bit different on different screens.

Care

Keep it out of extreme heat and don't drop it on hard floors. Glow-in-the-dark cases glow best after time in bright light.
